# Submission note

Crystal's brands read like seven separate businesses, so I organised them by what she does: **Build** (Studio COKA, ELEvated), **Teach** (The Effective Architect, Speaking, Research & Writing) and **Give** (AKO Alliance, Alive and Free). Studio COKA leads because it is the flagship with the strongest proof.

The page answers the brief's four questions in order. The hero states who she is and offers four routes chosen by intent ("I'm here to..."). A "Find the right door" section closes the page with one next step per visitor type. Real photography is labelled Completed, Visualisation or Concept, and missing brand links are clearly marked as placeholders.

Design draws on her own identity: the logo files themselves, cream and ink from the logo sheet, and an earth tone from the studio's materials.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion and Lenis, statically exported and served from Cloudflare's edge. Images are pre-sized to WebP at build time. Lighthouse scores 100 for accessibility, best practices and SEO. Every call to action sends a named analytics event.
