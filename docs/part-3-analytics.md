# Part 3: Analytics and improvement

**What I would track.** Enquiries and their sources first, since that is the site's job. Then the journey: which route visitors choose, call-to-action clicks, scroll depth, outbound clicks to each brand, and real-user Core Web Vitals.

**Tools.** Google Analytics 4 with key events per enquiry type, Search Console for queries and indexing, Cloudflare Web Analytics for real-user performance, and Microsoft Clarity for recordings and heatmaps. This site already sends a named event for every call to action.

**Using the data.** Monthly: find where visitors drop off, change one thing, measure it against the previous period.

**Scenario: 5,000 visitors, 5 enquiries (0.1%).** First I would confirm the number is real: test every form and mailto link, and check that the enquiry event fires. Then look at who the 5,000 are. If most arrive from Instagram on mobile, look at mobile first. If they arrive on journal articles, they came to read, not to hire, and those pages need a next step.

Next, follow the path. Do visitors reach the enquiry section, and where do they leave? Recordings show hesitation: long forms, unclear pricing, slow pages, no reassurance about process.

Then act: put one clear call to action per audience above the fold, shorten the form, add proof (completed projects, energy savings, client words), offer a lower-commitment step such as a downloadable brief or a consultation booking, and make WhatsApp contact available, since many Nigerian clients prefer it. Re-measure after four weeks.
